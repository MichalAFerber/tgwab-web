# Miniflux: The Feed Reader That Survived

Most "self-hosting 101" lists skip RSS or bury it under a three-column reader with AI summaries. I still want a list of feeds, keyboard to the next unread, done. [Miniflux](https://miniflux.app/) is that list.

It runs as two containers on the same Docker host as Omada and Homebox: `miniflux/miniflux:2.3.2` and Postgres 16. Pinned. Migrations on start. Admin created from env, not a public signup page I forgot was open. The database is on an internal network. The app sits on the proxy network. That is the whole topology.

## Why this one

I have tried the ones that want to be a social network. They grow a timeline. Miniflux stays a queue. Unread is a number. I knock it down.

Fever, FreshRSS, Tiny Tiny RSS — fine products. I already had Compose muscle and a preference for small Go binaries. Miniflux is one process plus Postgres. I did not want SQLite until I had a backup story I trusted for it; Postgres on this host already had one.

## The coat of paint

Stock dark mode is readable. I still wanted it to look like the rest of the desk. [miniflux-selfhst-theme](https://github.com/MichalAFerber/miniflux-selfhst-theme) is MIT, public, a custom CSS file. Near-black, blue accents, loosely the [selfh.st](https://selfh.st) palette, Iosevka. Paste it into Settings → Custom CSS. I am not going to pretend a theme is a product. It is how I stand to look at a thousand unread items.

## What it is not

It is not a replacement for [the notification stack](/2026-06-22-gatus-herald/). Feeds are not pages. It is not Miniflux-as-a-search-engine. If a site has no feed I do not scrape it into one.

Hostnames stay off this page. The shape is the post: **a queue, Postgres, a pin, a theme.** If I replace it, it will be because I stopped reading, not because a new reader has cards.
