# Homebox: Serials in a Box, Not a Spreadsheet

The house has access points, a switch, a NAS, a media mini, warranties in a drawer, and a brain that will not hold "which EAP is the hallway." A spreadsheet works until it does not. [Homebox](https://homebox.software/) is the inventory app that stayed.

`ghcr.io/sysadminsmedia/homebox:0.26.2`. Pinned. Data in a volume. Analytics off. It sits on the same Docker host as Miniflux and the Omada controller — not because they are one system, because that VM is where compose stacks live.

## What I put in it

Gear. Serials. Warranty dates. "This is the spare." Photos of the label on the bottom of a thing I will not turn over again. The homelab is the obvious customer. The household is the one that actually pays off: the appliance you cannot name at 9 p.m. when it dies.

I am not cataloging every cable. If it has a serial or a date that costs money when I lose it, it goes in.

## What I did not want

A CMDB. Snipe-IT is a good product and a bigger job than "where is the receipt." Grocy is groceries. Homebox is stuff. I did not run the other two first to make a comparison table. I needed a box, I picked one, it is still there.

Registration exists on the container. That is a lab default I treat as "people in the house," not an invite on the public internet. It is not a SaaS.

## Why it survived

Because I open it. The [self-hosting 101](/2026-06-16-self-hosting-101/) rule was weekly use or it leaves. Homebox is how I stop asking the hallway AP to identify itself. That is enough.

Hostnames stay off this page. Serial numbers stay in the app. The post is the shape: **one inventory, pinned, analytics off, not a spreadsheet I dread.**
